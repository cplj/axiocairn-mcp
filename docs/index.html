Choose the question

  • get_todays_reports: find reports for today or a specified date.
  • get_latest_report_by_type: read the newest available report in a selected family.
  • search_report_archive: find published reports or samples.
  • get_report_schedule: check expected times and generation records.
  • get_symbol_reports: list existing ticker reports, then retrieve a selected type.
  • get_house_view: check this week's House View and its next expected date.

Example: “What reports are available for today, and what are their data dates?” Tool discovery requires authorization. The server's current response governs availability.

Account access and data time

Sign in once when prompted and review the requested access. Reader is the free tier, with limited look-ups, delayed briefs, and limited history. Access follows your account permissions; see the current plan details.

Check each report's data cutoff separately from its publication date. Weekends and market holidays retain the latest trading day's data, so today's report may be absent. Expected publication times do not establish availability.

Scope and limits

This repository provides documentation and metadata for a hosted service. It does not include the server implementation. It describes retrieval of existing reports and makes no claim that report generation on request is available. The connection does not place trades or manage accounts.

Registry name: com.axiocairn/mcp. The metadata uses the official 2025-12-11 schema. Metadata here does not establish a Registry listing.

Axiocairn provides research information, not investment advice. Content is general, is not personalized, and is not a recommendation to buy or sell. Reports may be incomplete or delayed.
